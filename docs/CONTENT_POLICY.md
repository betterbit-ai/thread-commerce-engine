# Content Policy

## Voice and evidence

Write concise Korean as an experienced IT worker focused on long work sessions: useful, specific, and candid—not corporate affiliate spam. One main idea, a verified insight/caveat, offer-code CTA, then disclosure. Avoid hashtag/emoji spam.

Warmup posts use natural banmal and may ask for personal experiences around work and daily discomfort. They must not diagnose, recommend treatment, name a provider, or give exercise/medical instructions.

Unknown compatibility, ratings, reviews, discounts, scarcity, commission, and health effects remain unknown. “Who should not buy this” is encouraged only when based on verified specifications, explicit comparisons, or curated founder experience.

## Founder experience

`data/experience/founder.yml` is human-curated. A first-person use claim is allowed only when the exact `product_key` has `used: true` and observations. Codex/LLMs must never populate observations. Without evidence, phrases including `내가 써봤는데`, `내돈내산`, `직접 샀는데`, `회사에서 계속 쓰는데`, and equivalent claims hard-fail.

## Medical boundary

The account is not medical advice. Claims that equipment cures/prevents pain, carpal tunnel syndrome, injury, or posture problems hard-fail. Comfort-related product positioning must remain non-medical and evidence-limited.

## Deterministic hard fails

Fabricated experience; unsupported fact; unsupported health claim; missing/exactly changed disclosure; false scarcity; unsupported price superlative; near duplicate over configured threshold; broken/missing affiliate URL; unknown campaign mapping; or policy violation. Quality scores cannot override any failure.

## Disclosure

The configured Korean disclosure is appended by code and shown on every applicable storefront page. Do not ask the LLM to remember it or abbreviate it.
